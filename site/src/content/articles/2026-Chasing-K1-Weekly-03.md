The third edition of the weekly letter, and the first written in a bye week: Seoul have the bye in Round 30, and Yongin on October 31 is next. The [Chasing K1](/articles/2026-chasing-k1-promotion) page stays as the running summary; this is the diary.

## The number this week

**26 percent.** That is the model's chance, after 20,000 simulated finishes from the complete Round 29 table, that Seoul E-Land finish first or second and go up without a playoff. Suwon FC are at 74 percent, Daegu at 2. Suwon Samsung win the league in 89 percent of runs and finish in the top two in 98.5 percent.

The biggest rise since this table began, and it came on a weekend when Suwon FC won. A week ago the model said a Suwon FC win at Seongnam would drop Seoul to about 11 percent. Suwon FC did win, and Seoul's chance went up by eight points anyway. The model does not split the change into parts, but three of its inputs moved Seoul's way: the tiebreak, Seoul's own form, and the form of the clubs left to play. Two of them run through one wet afternoon at Mokdong.

## The weekend: Round 29, October 17 and 18

**Seoul E-Land 6-2 Gimhae.** Heavy rain, a waterlogged pitch, and a goal down after 20 minutes to the bottom club. Then Park Jae-yong (24), Alan Carius with the club's 600th goal (28), Euller (52), Son Hyuk-chan with his first career goal (60), Euller again from a direct free kick (85) and Kim Hyun (90). Six equals the club's single-match scoring record; it was a third straight win and Kim Do-gyun's 50th win as Seoul E-Land manager. How the strikers and the two tall center-backs carried it is in [Twenty-Two Goals Up Front, Twin Towers at the Back](/articles/2026-strikers-and-twin-towers).

The goals changed the arithmetic. Suwon FC's lead on goals scored, the first tiebreaker, went from 51-46 to 53-52, which Kim Do-gyun called a positive sign. A week ago, when the two finished level on points in the model, Seoul came out ahead less than 5 percent of the time; now it is 39 percent. A level finish is still unlikely (about 9 percent of runs), but it is no longer close to a certain loss.

**Seongnam 1-2 Suwon FC.** Han Chan-hee scored on 37, Yoo Ju-an equalized on 84, and Gu Bon-cheol won it in the 96th minute. Before the round, Suwon FC failing to win there was worth 27 percent to Seoul; the equalizer lasted twelve minutes. Fifteen unbeaten.

**Suwon Samsung 3-2 Hwaseong.** A sixth straight win, five points clear, and 89 percent for the title, up from 83.

**Daegu 3-1 Chungbuk Cheongju.** Back from the bye, still five behind second with five to play: 2 percent for the top two, 82 percent to finish fourth.

Elsewhere, Gimpo beat Paju 4-1 and Chungnam Asan drew 0-0 at Gyeongnam. Gimpo, seventh on 38, can now reach 53 at most, so Seoul are mathematically certain of a playoff place. [The Long Way Up](/articles/2026-the-playoff-route) explains how this season's playoffs work.

A prediction circulating online puts Seoul above 50 percent for the top two and Suwon FC in the mid-40s. We could not find its source in Korean or English; the longer answer is on the [Chasing K1](/articles/2026-chasing-k1-promotion#higher-numbers-you-may-have-seen) page. In short: Suwon FC are level on points with a game in hand and still lead on goals scored, and even four wins from four sends Seoul up only 70 percent of the time in our model. Ours is an estimate too, like any model's, and not the only way to read the race.

## What Seoul's next match does to the number

Round 31 is Yongin away on Saturday October 31 at 14:00. The model gives Seoul 70 percent to win, 19 to draw and 11 to lose, with expected goals of 2.28 to 0.78, 2-0 the likeliest score and a clean sheet in 46 percent of runs. Yongin are 13th and lost 4-0 at home to Ansan on Saturday; the first meeting, at Mokdong in May, was a 2-2 draw. Seoul will be without Son Hyuk-chan, whose booking against Gimhae was his fifth of the season.

A win takes the number to a third; a draw roughly halves it. Suwon FC play Ansan the next day, so this time Seoul go first. Seoul's win chances have risen across the run-in since last week: Yongin from 49 percent to 70, Jeonnam at home from 69 to 80, Chungnam Asan at home from 55 to 63, Gyeongnam away from 36 to 41. Most of that is form: the model blends each club's last five matches into its season rates, and Seoul's last five now read 11 goals scored and 4 conceded, Yongin's 3 and 10.

## What Seoul need from here

Four matches; the possible totals run from 54 to 66, and the model projects 62.6. Seoul go up 24 percent of the time at 63 (three wins and a defeat), 38 percent at 64 (three wins and a draw) and 70 percent at 66, four from four, which happens in about 15 percent of runs. Kim Do-gyun put it more simply after Gimhae: Seoul have to win every remaining match, and that is where the hope is.

With the tiebreak gap at one, the goals count too: a 2-0 at Yongin, the model's likeliest score, is worth more than a 1-0. The win first, then the goals, starting at Yongin on Saturday October 31, 14:00.
