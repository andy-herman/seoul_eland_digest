## Round 30, October 24 and 25: the week Seoul did not play

**Seoul E-Land had the bye**, their second of the season.

**Suwon FC 0-1 Chungnam Asan.** A Suwon FC own goal on 26 minutes ended a 15-match unbeaten run, and the game in hand they had held all autumn was gone. Before the round the model had made Suwon FC 52 percent to win it.

**Seongnam 0-2 Suwon Samsung.** A seventh straight win for the leaders.

**Jeonnam 2-4 Daegu.** Daegu moved to two points behind second.

Net effect: Seoul from 23 percent to 34, Suwon FC from 74 to 51, Daegu from 5 to 16. For one week second place was close to a coin flip, and Seoul's next match was at the side in 13th.

## Round 31, October 31 and November 1: Yongin

**Yongin 4-2 Seoul E-Land.** The afternoon this letter will remember. Seoul had 68 percent of the ball, 19 shots and ten corners, and lost their captain Park Chang-hwan to an ankle injury after 21 minutes. They conceded four: the first on the break, the second from Caio's back pass, a penalty and a counter-attack in the tenth minute of stoppage time. Gabriel scored twice for Yongin, who had never won two league matches in a row before. Kim Do-gyun: "We lost with nothing to complain about." After Round 30 the model had made Seoul 45 percent to win; a win would have taken their chance to about half. The [Round 31 digest](/rounds/2026-r31_seoul_e-land_digest) has the match.

**Daegu 2-2 Suwon Samsung.** In front of 12,230, the leaders' winning run ended at seven.

**Suwon FC 1-0 Ansan.** On the Sunday, the day after Seoul's defeat, and back to three points clear.

Net effect: Seoul from 34 percent to 13, Suwon FC from 51 to 77, Daegu from 16 to 11.

## Round 32, November 7: Jeonnam

**Seoul E-Land 3-2 Jeonnam.** A goal down on 15 minutes, level from the penalty spot on 42, ahead from the spot again on 52 after a VAR review, and 3-1 on 72 through Park Jae-yong before Jeonnam pulled one back. Euller's two penalties take him to 13 goals, his best season. Kim Do-gyun said he was not satisfied and that luck had helped. The [Round 32 digest](/rounds/2026-r32_seoul_e-land_digest) has the match.

**Suwon FC 3-0 Gyeongnam.** Earlier the same afternoon.

**Seongnam 0-1 Daegu.** Also on Saturday.

**Still to come on Sunday:** Suwon Samsung v Yongin, where a draw clinches promotion for the leaders, plus Cheonan v Busan and Paju v Chungnam Asan.

Net effect: Seoul from 13 percent to 8, Suwon FC from 77 to 86, Daegu from 11 to 6. All three won, so the gaps did not move, but the same three-point gap with two matches left is worth more to Suwon FC than it was with three.

## The race for third

With second slipping away, the place that is genuinely open is third. Seoul are on 57 and Daegu on 56, and Daegu have scored one more goal, 58 to 57, so if they finish level on points and the goal totals stay as they are, Daegu go above. The model has Seoul third in 43 percent of runs and fourth in 49; Daegu are third in 46 and fourth in 48.

It matters because of how the playoffs work. Third plays sixth in the semi-finals on December 2 and fourth plays fifth, both at the higher seed's ground, with the higher seed going through on a draw. Third also hosts the final on December 5 if they get there, again with a draw enough for the home side. And even the loser of the final gets one more chance, a playoff against K League 1's bottom club, so this year up to four K League 2 clubs can go up. [The Long Way Up](/articles/2026-the-playoff-route) has the details.

Daegu's last two are Gimhae at home, which the model makes 73 percent Daegu, and Cheonan away. Seoul's are Chungnam Asan at home and Gyeongnam away.

## What Seoul need from here

Two matches; the possible totals run from 57 to 63, and the model projects 60.3. Second place needs two wins, which happen in 19 percent of runs, and then Suwon FC to drop at least three of their six points: if they win one and lose one, it goes to goals scored, where the two clubs are level on 57 and Seoul come out above about seven times in ten. Third place needs Seoul to stay ahead of Daegu, which in practice means matching their results. Kim Do-gyun said on Saturday that the win had at least kept Seoul in a position to keep chasing. The chase starts against the side that beat them 3-0 in May.
