Part 15 covered [Kang Hyeon-je](/articles/2026-season-review-kang-hyeon-je). Part 16 is the left-back who has spent the season half in the team. Bae Seo-jun has started eight league matches, finished one, and is the name in our predicted back four for Gimpo on October 9.

## The season in one paragraph

Bae arrived from Daejeon in June 2025, a left-footed full-back who had started every match of Korea's run to the 2023 U-20 World Cup semifinals and had not played a league minute for Daejeon that spring. He said when he signed that Seoul had struck him as organized and intimidating when he faced them with Suwon Samsung, and that he would give everything to get the team promoted. He played 16 times in the second half of 2025. In 2026 he has 13 appearances, 657 minutes, no goals and one assist, the through ball for Carius's equalizer against Cheonan in July. He came off the bench five times in the spring, was out of the matchday squad from mid-May to late July, won the left-back place when the back four returned, and then kept losing it at halftime. He turned 22 in December, so he no longer counts toward the U-22 rule.

## What he has done well

**He gives the ball back to his own team.** Eighty-five percent of his passes find a teammate and 58 percent of his long balls do, which puts him near the top of FotMob's comparison group on both. Kim Do-gyun's 4-3-3 builds through Osmar and Baek Ji-woong and asks the full-backs to keep it moving. At Cheongju in September, the 2-0 win that ended with a clean sheet, Bae played 89 minutes on the left, rated 7.1, and was only replaced by Caio Marcelo in stoppage time.

**Cheonan, July 24.** Seoul were 1-0 down at Mokdong after 12 minutes. Four minutes later Bae slid a through ball from the flank into Carius's run, and Carius took it at speed and finished over Lee Seung-gyu. It started a 4-3 win on the night Kim Do-gyun went back to a back four. Bae took a heavy challenge on the ankle before halftime, played on, and it remains his only full 90.

**He creates from a defensive position.** Nine chances created in fewer than 600 FotMob minutes is a high rate, and it comes from the same habit as the Carius pass: he looks forward first.

**He came back from six matches out.** Bae was absent from the matchday squad from Round 12 to Round 18, with no injury reported. He returned as a starter against Cheonan and kept the place for seven of the next eight rounds, which is the right answer from a player who had already sat out half a year at Daejeon.

## Where the same mistakes keep showing up

**He loses the physical contests.** At 173 centimeters he has won 29.8 percent of his duels and three of ten in the air, a duel rate FotMob puts in the bottom few percent of its comparison group. The matches show it. At Hwaseong in August he spent the first half in collisions with Kim Dae-hwan and Lee Jong-sung, held his chest after the second, and was off two minutes into the second half. Against Paju a fortnight later he was booked on 40 minutes and did not come out for the second half.

**He does not finish matches.** Thirteen appearances, one full 90. He was taken off at halftime at Chungnam Asan, against Ansan, against Paju and against Suwon Samsung, and at 47 minutes at Hwaseong. Some of that is design: the Suwon change was one of two the manager said he had spent two days preparing, and Son Hyuk-chan is his standard way of chasing a game down the left. But a player taken off at or around halftime in five of eight starts is a starter the manager is not yet sure of. When Kim Do-gyun rotated five players at Seongnam, Son started instead, and against Daegu, in a 3-4-3, Bae did not get off the bench.

**Two seasons, no goals.** Twenty-nine league appearances for Seoul without scoring. A full-back does not need goals, but one whose case rests on his attacking play needs more than one assist.

## His strengths, and what they mean for the last six

**Possession against low blocks.** Gimpo, Gimhae, Yongin, Jeonnam, Chungnam Asan and Gyeongnam will mostly sit in. A left-back who keeps the ball and looks for the forward pass is more useful against that than a pure defender, which is the case for Bae starting at Gimpo, where our preview has him behind Ahn Joo-wan.

**The Ahn Joo-wan link.** The Carius pass at Cheonan is the template. If Bae can put the ball into the channel early for the quickest runner Seoul have left, the left side becomes Seoul's quickest route to goal against deep defenses.

**The Son Hyuk-chan partnership, used properly.** The realistic plan is Bae for an hour against a side that is sitting in, Son when the game turns physical or Seoul need to chase. That is how Kim Do-gyun has used them since August.

## The verdict at Round 27

A tidy, forward-thinking full-back who has not yet shown he can hold a starting place through a whole match, let alone a whole season. The assist at Cheonan, the passing numbers and the Cheongju clean sheet are real. So are the duel numbers, the five halftime exits and the two months out of the squad.

**Grade so far: C plus.** A useful squad player who has done his job in about half of each match he has started. The plus is for Cheonan and Cheongju.
