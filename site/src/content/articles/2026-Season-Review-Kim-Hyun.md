Part 7 covered [Oh In-pyo](/articles/2026-season-review-oh-in-pyo), the wing-back who keeps turning up in the box. Part 8 is the striker Kim Do-gyun sends on when he wants the ball in the box by the most direct route available. Kim Hyun has been Park Jae-yong's alternative all season, and his year is best read through the matches he started and the ones he changed from the bench.

## The season in one paragraph

Kim Hyun joined on a free transfer in January, his ninth K League club, after two seasons at Suwon Samsung that ended with a promotion playoff defeat to Jeju. He said plainly why he came: "It was almost entirely because of Kim Do-gyun," the manager he played for at Suwon FC in 2022, when he scored a career-high eight league goals. He was named vice-captain before a ball was kicked, told reporters he did not assume he was a starter, and then had his nose broken by an elbow on the opening day at his old club. Through Round 27 he has 21 appearances, six starts and 814 minutes, one goal and no assists. He started six times with Park Jae-yong on the bench, and Seoul won four of those. In three of the four wins, the man who came on for him made the difference.

## What he has done well

**He played through a broken nose.** On February 28 at Suwon, in front of a record crowd and the club he had just left, Hong Won-jin's elbow caught him in an aerial duel and he came off the pitch bleeding. Kim Do-gyun said afterward the nose injury was serious and needed checking. A week later he was on the bench against Gyeongnam in a mask, and came on at halftime. The manager's summary was short: "Hyun is fine. His nose is broken, but he plays in a mask." It set the tone for what his season would be: minutes when asked, no complaints about how many.

**The goal was worth waiting for.** Kim Do-gyun started him against Yongin in May partly because Park Jae-yong looked tired, and said he hoped Kim would finally score his first. It took until June 7. On 33 minutes against Chungbuk Cheongju, Kim Hyun-woo's cross from the left ran through to Oh In-pyo, the goalkeeper parried Oh's shot, and Kim Hyun met the rebound with a scissor volley into the net. He also had two turning shots blocked and fed Oh In-pyo for Park Chang-hwan's chance. He went off injured on 76, and Seoul then conceded twice late to the bottom side. The goal deserved a better evening.

**He changes the shape of the attack from the bench.** Fifteen of his 21 appearances have been as a substitute, and the best of them read the same way. At Paju in August he came on for Ahn Joo-wan on 58, and inside a minute his header from Carius's early cross forced a save and Park Jae-yong buried the rebound. Kim Do-gyun explained afterward that he had asked for crosses "because of his height, and it worked as soon as he went on." At Seongnam on 73 he headed down for Osmar's drive, which drew the save of the round. Against Suwon Samsung on 84 his header was the closest Seoul came. At Chungnam Asan in May his header from Euller's free kick hit the post.

**He wins the ball in the air.** Thirty-seven aerial duels won in 814 minutes puts him in the top tenth of K League 2 forwards per 90, and he takes shots at a rate few strikers in the division match, more than four per 90. He described his own game in January as mostly about goals from crosses. The numbers agree.

## Where the same mistakes keep showing up

**One goal from 31 shots.** Ten on target, eleven headers, one goal. The manager said it publicly after Paju: that Kim Hyun had been playing but had not found the key to scoring, that he had had several chances, and that "he will know that himself." The same evening he headed over, unmarked, from Gabriel's cross. At Mokdong in September against Suwon Samsung, with Seoul chasing a 1-0, his header went at the goalkeeper. For a player brought in to add goals from crosses, that is the part of the brief that has not been met.

**His starts get fixed by his replacement.** Gimhae in July: he started as the lone striker, nearly scored with a half-volley from distance, and was withdrawn on 56 for Park Jae-yong, who scored and assisted in a 3-1 win. Cheonan: off at halftime, Park on and scoring in a 4-3. Ansan in August: off at halftime with the score 1-1, and the second half, with Park on, was the comfortable one. Yongin in May he led the line for an hour and, in the digest's words, lacked that extra beat of movement on crosses. When he starts, Seoul have tended to look like a side waiting for its second half.

**Not quite enough in the channels.** Two successful dribbles all season. He links play when he has his back to goal, but he rarely carries the ball himself, and against a set defense he needs the delivery to come to him.

## His strengths, and what they mean for the last six

**The late aerial option.** Kim Do-gyun said after Daegu that goals scored could decide second place, with Seoul level on points with Suwon FC and five behind on that tiebreaker, and that his substitutes need to contribute more of them. All six remaining opponents are bottom-half sides likely to sit deep. Kim Hyun alongside or in place of Park Jae-yong for the last half hour, with crosses from Euller, Carius and Oh In-pyo, is the most direct version of that plan the squad has.

**Familiar opponents.** He started at home to Yongin and away at Gimhae, both on the run-in, and his header hit the post against Chungnam Asan, who come to Mokdong in Round 33.

**The dressing room.** He is a 33-year-old vice-captain in a squad built around players in their early twenties, and he said in January that he came because this had to be the year Seoul went up. On the evidence of March, when he played with a broken nose, he will do whatever minutes he is asked to do.

## The verdict at Round 27

Kim Hyun has done the job of a backup striker without doing the one thing that would have made him more than that. The minutes have been honest and the aerial work real: Park's equalizer at Paju started with his header, and Osmar's best chance at Seongnam came off his knockdown. One goal in 21 appearances is still one goal. He has six matches to turn a useful season into a decisive one, and the plan for those matches suits him.

**Grade so far: C plus.** A dependable substitute, a vice-captain and a genuine aerial threat. The return in front of goal keeps it out of the B range.
