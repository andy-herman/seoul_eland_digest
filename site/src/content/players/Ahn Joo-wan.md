# Ahn Joo-wan


## About

Made his professional debut for Seoul E-Land at 16 years, 11 months, 7 days old in Round 4 (away vs Cheonan, 2026-03-21), setting the all-time K League 2 youngest-appearance record. Substituted on in the second half. High-school national-team prospect coming through E-Land's youth pathway.
