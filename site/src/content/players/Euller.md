## About

Brazilian forward and Seoul E-Land's MVP / talisman. Re-signed a three-year contract during the offseason. 2025 stat line: 12 goals, 11 assists. Versatile across the front line, used as right-sided wide forward, left, or central. Took the R10 penalty (saved + retake awarded for keeper movement, then converted) for his first goal of 2026.
