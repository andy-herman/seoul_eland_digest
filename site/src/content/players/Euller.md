# Euller (에울레르)


## About

Brazilian forward and Seoul E-Land's MVP / talisman. Re-signed a three-year contract during the offseason. Arrived from Marítimo in January 2025 with no prior K League minutes. 2025 stat line: 12 goals, 11 assists (won K League 2 Top Assist Award, named to Best XI). Versatile across the front line, used as right-sided wide forward, left, or central. Took the R10 penalty (saved + retake awarded for keeper movement, then converted) for his first goal of 2026. Also the regular set-piece taker.
