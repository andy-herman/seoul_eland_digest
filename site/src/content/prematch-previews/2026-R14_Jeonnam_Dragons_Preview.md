## Strategy That Can Work

The tactical plan should be active and patient at the same time. Jeonnam are unlikely to dominate possession; they are likely to absorb it and look for one or two transitions. Seoul need to make the early phases of the match expensive for them.

- Break the block early. Jeonnam have lost six of nine; their organization is not built to defend a deficit. An early Seoul goal forces them to come out of their shape and play the chasing version of the game they have struggled with all season.
- Win the set-piece exchange. Euller and Park Jae-yong have produced repeatedly from dead-ball situations this spring. A clean set-piece game is the most reliable route to the first goal and the cleanest way to manage the away crowd.
- Manage the back three through Osmar's absence. If Kim Oh-kyu starts, the central defending duties shift to a veteran who has not started in several rounds. Communication and depth on aerial deliveries are the obvious worry against any tall Jeonnam forward.
- Do not let Park Jae-yong cool. Five goals from 13, just rewarded with the starting role, momentum is on his side. Repeated central service should be the default rather than the exception.
