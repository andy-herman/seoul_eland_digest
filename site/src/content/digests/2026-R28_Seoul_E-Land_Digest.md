# Round 28 Digest: Seoul E-Land FC vs Gimpo Citizen

> *Twenty shots, one goal, and it was enough: Euller's first since his August hat-trick, set up by a substitute on 66 minutes, beat Gimpo 1-0 on a holiday afternoon at Solteo, kept Seoul level on points with Suwon FC, and left the finishing problem exactly where Kim Do-gyun said it was.*

## The Round in One Paragraph

Seoul E-Land won 1-0 at Gimpo in front of 2,181 on Hangul Day, their fourth straight win at Gimpo and their second in a row, and they did it the hard way after a first half that should have been put away. They had 64 percent of the ball before the interval, Son Jeong-hyeon made the save of the afternoon from [[Park Chang-hwan]] with his feet, and it was still 0-0. Kim Do-gyun sent on [[Alan Carius]] at halftime and then, on 59, [[Kim Hyun]] and [[Son Hyuk-chan]] for [[Cho Jun-hyun]] and [[Kim Joo-hwan]], moving to two strikers; seven minutes later Kim Hyun found [[Euller]] on the right and Euller shifted the ball onto his left foot and curled it in for his ninth of the season. Gimpo threw everything at the last fifteen minutes and [[Min Sung-jun]], Son Hyuk-chan and [[Osmar]] kept the clean sheet. Suwon FC had beaten Hwaseong 1-0 at lunchtime, so Seoul stay third on 51 points, level with Suwon FC and five goals behind them on the tiebreaker, two points off Suwon Samsung with five matches left. Their manager's summary: the run-in "really dries the blood."

## Match Report

Kim Do-gyun went back to the 4-3-3 after the 3-4-3 that beat Daegu, and the reason was standing in the middle of it: Park Chang-hwan, back from his suspension and wearing the armband. The captain's armband was free because [[Kim Oh-kyu]] was not in the matchday squad at all. The broadcast said he was out with a knee problem; the club, which had announced his contract renewal the day before alongside Osmar's, said nothing about an injury, and no timeline has been given. The eleven was Min Sung-jun; [[Oh In-pyo]], [[Baek Ji-woong]], Osmar and Kim Joo-hwan across the back; Park Chang-hwan, [[Yoon Seok-ju]] and Cho Jun-hyun in midfield; Euller, [[Park Jae-yong]] and [[Lee Ju-hyeok]] in front. [[Caio Marcelo]], who started against Daegu, was back on the bench. Ko Jeong-woon's Gimpo lined up in a 3-4-3 with Paulinho, Lee Si-heon and Park Dong-jin up front, and without Luis, sold to Suwon Samsung in August, and without Luan and Chapman, neither of whom was in the squad. Ko had said before the match that Luis and Luan had scored exactly half of Gimpo's league goals between them. Ko had said before kickoff that he would drop his line, hold out and counter. That is exactly what Gimpo did, and for an hour it nearly worked.

The first half was a siege with no breach. Seoul had 68 percent of the ball in the first half hour and the chances to match. Yoon Seok-ju forced an early save, Euller's crosses kept dropping into a crowded box, and when Son Jeong-hyeon punched one out, Cho Jun-hyun's first-time follow-up flew over. Twice the broadcast singled out Lee Hak-min for last-ditch interventions, which tells you how close the traffic was getting. Paulinho was booked on 16 for bringing down Park Chang-hwan as he broke out of midfield, the only card of the match. The best chance came a few minutes later and it was a lovely one: Cho Jun-hyun let a pass run through to Park Chang-hwan, who took a good touch and finished well, and Son got down to block it with his feet. Osmar hit a left-footed shot wide from the second phase of a corner. Gimpo went more than twenty minutes without a shot, and their two moments of the half came from Park Dong-jin, whose deflected shot Osmar blocked, and from a two-on-two break that ended with Paulinho dragging a left-footer wide. In the second minute of stoppage time Euller switched play, the ball reached Park Jae-yong in the box, and his shot was stopped. Nil-nil, and the broadcast's verdict from midway through the half still held: Seoul were running the match without hurting Gimpo enough.

Kim Do-gyun's first change came at halftime, Carius for Lee Ju-hyeok. The commentators thought Lee had taken a knock in the first half; the manager explained it afterward in purely tactical terms, saying that with Carius and Euller on opposite flanks the attack is spread too wide for the opponent to cover, and that he had told Carius to roam into the half-space as well as hug the line. Carius was in the game within minutes, cutting back inside and crossing for Euller, whose attempt went wide with the flag already up. Gimpo dropped even deeper. Their captain Kim Tae-han went down after a heavy challenge and got up again. Seoul kept circulating, and kept not scoring.

On 59 Kim Do-gyun made the move that won the match. Kim Hyun came on for Cho Jun-hyun and Son Hyuk-chan for Kim Joo-hwan, which put Kim Hyun alongside Park Jae-yong and turned the shape into something close to a 4-4-2, a 4-2-4 when Seoul had the ball. The broadcast called it the twin towers, and the set pieces immediately got more dangerous: Son saved a header from Osmar and then dealt with a Park Jae-yong attempt as Seoul piled corners into the box. The goal, though, came from open play. On 66 Seoul won the ball high up the pitch after a Gimpo pass was cut out, Kim Hyun played it wide to Euller on the right of the area, and Euller did the thing he has been doing for two seasons: a touch with the right foot to shift it across his marker, then a quick left-footed curler that Son could not reach. Kim Hyun said on the broadcast afterward that the team had rehearsed exactly this in training, because after a turnover the space on Euller's side is always open. Ko Jeong-woon said his side had defended Euller's flank well all afternoon. They had, for 65 minutes.

Gimpo had to come out after that, and the last twenty minutes were the most uncomfortable Seoul have spent since the Daegu siege. Ko changed four players in two waves, Kim Do-hyeok and Im Chang-seok on 69 for Dizauma and Lee Hak-min, then Kim Jun-seo and Kim Min-sik on 81 for Lee Si-heon and Kim Sung-jun, and his team had 53 percent of the ball over the final quarter hour. Seoul still had the better of the chances for a while: Park Chang-hwan got in behind and crossed, and Park Jae-yong could not make clean contact, and Carius beat his man and got a left-footed shot away. Ahn Joo-wan replaced Park Jae-yong on 82, with the broadcast expecting Carius to move inside and Seoul to go back to one striker. Then Gimpo found a very good pass through the middle and Son Hyuk-chan arrived from nowhere with a tackle on Paulinho that the broadcast rightly treated as the defensive moment of the match. Paulinho dragged a left-footed shot wide from the edge of the area soon after. On 87 Caio came on for Euller to add another body at the back. Gimpo crossed from both sides. Min Sung-jun made a sharp save from a quick shot, and from the corners that followed Gimpo sent a header over and had a second-ball shot blocked. Osmar, the broadcast noted, had made a great many clearances. The whistle came well into stoppage time, and Kim Do-gyun admitted afterward that his players had looked physically spent at the end.

## Player Performances

**[[Euller]] (에울레르):**
Ninth league goal of the season, one short of a second straight season in double figures, and his first since the Round 22 hat-trick against Ansan on August 16. Kim Do-gyun was unusually frank about the drought: his attacking numbers are down on last year, he said, and you could see him getting hurried in matches; he hopes Euller stops worrying about it, and added that his team play, defensive work and sacrifice have given nobody anything to criticize. FourFourTwo Korea counted 17 league matches in which Euller has scored for Seoul and found the club unbeaten in all of them, thirteen wins and four draws.

**[[Kim Hyun]] (김현):**
Sent on at 59 minutes, he changed the match in seven. The assist was his first of the season, and the broadcaster made him its player of the match. He is now three appearances short of 300 in the K League, and he said on the broadcast that in all those years he has never been promoted with any club, so promotion this year is the only thing he is looking at. He plays shadow striker, midfielder and second forward depending on the week, and on Friday the second-forward version won the game.

**[[Son Hyuk-chan]] (손혁찬):**
Came on at left-back with Seoul still trying to score and finished it defending a lead against a team with nothing to lose. The tackle on Paulinho, timed perfectly as a very good Gimpo pass went through, is the reason Seoul won rather than drew. He is still on four bookings and still one from a ban, which makes a tackle like that braver than it looks.

**[[Osmar]] (오스마르):**
Renewed on Thursday, immaculate on Friday. Blocked Park Dong-jin's shot in the first half, had a header saved and a left-footed shot off target at the other end, and spent the last fifteen minutes heading everything Gimpo threw into the box back out of it. He is chasing Dejan's record for appearances by a foreign player in the K League, and on this evidence he will get there.

**[[Park Chang-hwan]] (박창환):**
Back from his ban with the armband and the energy of a player who had spent a week watching. Drew the only booking of the match by being too quick for Paulinho, had the best chance of the first half, and was still getting in behind in the last twenty minutes. His pre-match line was that the first game after the break was one Seoul had to win. He made sure of the "had to" part.

**Son Jeong-hyeon (손정현, Gimpo):**
Five saves, including the one with his feet from Park Chang-hwan, and he is not fit. His manager said afterward that Son cannot even take kicks at the moment and that with two other goalkeepers out long-term, Gimpo are grateful for everything he gives them. Without him this is 3-0.

## Fan Reaction

The image of the afternoon was the celebration. The broadcast picked out who Euller celebrated his goal with: Kim Oh-kyu, the captain who was not in the squad and who had been told, the day before, that the club wanted him for another season.

The away end was the other story. On a public holiday afternoon, Kim Hyun used the end of his broadcast interview to thank the supporters who follow the team to away grounds and to promise them the remaining matches would be repaid with wins and with promotion. It is the kind of thing players say. It is also the second consecutive match in which the person saying it had just decided the game.

Gimpo's crowd watched a club in a strange place. The embezzlement scandal, after which the chief executive resigned in August, is still unresolved, the city's audit findings are due this month, and the mayor said this week that a sale or dissolution has not been considered. The supporters' group Golden Crew had issued a statement in September demanding that the club be protected and the people responsible held to account. Their team had won its previous match against Busan. Ko Jeong-woon apologized to the fans afterward for not extending the run, and said he wanted to praise his players for refusing to give up.

The papers split along the lines you would expect. Starnews led on the run of four straight wins at Gimpo and on Kim Do-gyun's "dries the blood" line, with the manager saying that luck seems to follow Seoul whenever they come to Gimpo. The evening round-ups from Newsis and News1 set Seoul's win beside Suwon FC's, a fourteenth straight match unbeaten for the side that now holds second place on goals scored.

## Tactical and Strategic Watch

**The finishing problem is now on the record.** Twenty shots, six on target by the league's count, five saves from a goalkeeper who cannot kick the ball, and one goal. Kim Do-gyun did not pretend otherwise: there were one or two perfect chances in the first half, he said, the team creates plenty, and the finishing is disappointing and needs work on the training ground. He also pointed out that his team has been getting more shots and more shots on target since the break, which is true and is the right thing to say in public. In private, the tiebreaker is goals scored, and Seoul are exactly where they were before the break against Suwon FC: five behind.

**The 59-minute double change has become a pattern.** Against Daegu the change on 61 was a planned left-side swap. Against Gimpo it was a change of system, two strikers instead of one, and it produced the goal within seven minutes. The manager said after the Daegu match that the substitutes needed to contribute more goals. Three weeks later a substitute made the winner and another substitute made the tackle that kept it. Kim Hyun described his role this season as shadow striker, midfielder and second forward in rotation, which is a useful thing to have in a squad that is short of goals rather than short of chances.

**Carius and Euller on both flanks.** The halftime change put Seoul's two best ball-carriers on opposite sides, and the manager's stated reason was to spread the attack and stop the opponent sliding across to one side. Ko Jeong-woon's post-match comments suggest it worked: he said Gimpo's defending of Euller's side had been good, which suggests that is where they had concentrated their cover. Once Carius was on the other wing, that was harder to do.

**The last fifteen minutes are a concern.** Gimpo had more of the ball in the final segment and Seoul were hanging on by the end, which their manager admitted. That is now two matches in a row in which Seoul have finished the game defending deep against a team throwing bodies forward. Against Gimhae at home that should not matter. Against Gyeongnam away on the last day it might.

## My Honest Read

This was a match Seoul should have won by three and nearly drew, against a Gimpo side without its two leading scorers, playing with an unfit goalkeeper, inside a club that is fighting for its own survival off the pitch. The win is real and the clean sheet is real, and I would not read much more into either. What matters is the arithmetic: Suwon FC are fourteen unbeaten, level on points, five goals better off and a game in hand. Kim Do-gyun said it himself, that Seoul are the ones chasing and that hoping for a rival to slip is no plan. The only plan available is to win five more and score more goals doing it, and on Friday they managed one of those two things.
