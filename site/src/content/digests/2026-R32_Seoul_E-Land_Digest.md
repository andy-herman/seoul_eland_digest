# Round 32 Digest: Seoul E-Land FC vs Jeonnam Dragons

> *Two penalties from Euller and a rebound from Park Jae-yong: Seoul E-Land came from behind to beat Jeonnam 3-2 on the first cold day of winter at Mokdong, kept the race for second alive with two rounds left, and stopped Suwon Samsung clinching promotion on Saturday. Kim Do-gyun said he was not satisfied.*

## The Round in One Paragraph

Seoul E-Land beat Jeonnam Dragons 3-2 at [[Mokdong Stadium]] on Saturday, a week after losing 4-2 at Yongin, in front of 4,506 on a clear, cold afternoon. Jeong Ji-yong put Jeonnam ahead on 15 minutes from a rebound. [[Euller]] equalised from the penalty spot on 42 after Kim Ju-yeop pulled [[Osmar]] down at a corner, and scored a second penalty on 52 after a VAR review gave Kim Ju-yeop a handball. [[Park Jae-yong]] made it 3-1 on 72 from the rebound of an Euller shot, and Ronan pulled one back on 85 from Valdivia's pass. Euller's two goals take him to 13, the best season of his career. Seoul are third on 57 points, three behind Suwon FC, who beat Gyeongnam 3-0 earlier in the afternoon, and one ahead of Daegu, who won 1-0 at Seongnam. Seoul and Suwon FC are level on goals scored, the first tiebreaker, at 57 each. Two matches remain: Chungnam Asan at home on November 22 and Gyeongnam away on November 29.

## Match Report

Kim Do-gyun made four changes from Yongin and kept a back four, this time in a 4-3-3. [[Park Chang-hwan]] was out with the ankle injury he suffered there, Caio and [[Bae Seo-jun]] were not in the matchday squad, and [[Alan Carius]] dropped to the bench. [[Min Sung-jun]] was in goal behind [[Kim Joo-hwan]], Osmar, [[Baek Ji-woong]] and [[Oh In-pyo]], with Baek moving into the middle of the defence. [[Kang Hyeon-je]], [[Yoon Seok-ju]] and [[Cho Jun-hyun]] made up the midfield, and Gabriel, Park Jae-yong and Euller the front three. Osmar wore the armband. Lim Kwan-sik's Jeonnam played a 4-4-2 with goalkeeper and captain Choi Bong-jin, and Hanam and their ten-goal forward Valdivia up front.

Jeonnam started better. Min Sung-jun saved from Valdivia in the sixth minute, and on 15 Valdivia reached the byline on the left and cut the ball back; Min saved Park Tae-yong's shot, and Jeong Ji-yong put the rebound in with his left foot. Seoul's answer came mostly from Euller's left foot: a cross that Kang Hyeon-je headed just wide, and a corner that Osmar headed wide. Gabriel spent the half in a running physical contest with Kim Beom-su on the right of Jeonnam's defence.

The equaliser came from a set piece. At a Seoul corner on 39 minutes Kim Ju-yeop held Osmar and pulled him to the ground, and after a long conversation with the video room, referee Seo Dong-hwan gave the penalty. Euller hit it hard with his left foot on 42 and Choi Bong-jin, who had guessed right, could not keep it out. Hanam was booked in the aftermath, and Euller was booked in first-half stoppage time for a foul the broadcast described as away from the ball.

Kim Do-gyun changed two at half-time, [[Seo Jin-seok]] for Yoon Seok-ju and Carius for Gabriel, and seven minutes into the half the lead arrived. Cho Jun-hyun's shot struck Kim Ju-yeop's arm, by most accounts; the referee first gave a corner, then went to the pitchside monitor and gave the penalty. This time Euller waited, Choi went early, and the ball went the other way on 52. On 72 Euller cut in from the right and hit a left-footed shot that Choi saved, and Park Jae-yong was first to the rebound.

Jeonnam had made a double change on 57, Mirandinha and Ronan for Yoon Min-ho and Hanam, and the last 20 minutes were theirs. Min Sung-jun saved a dipping shot from Mirandinha late on, Euller went off on 84, and a minute later Ronan turned in Valdivia's pass. The goal was not given at first; footage showed the ball had crossed the line. Valdivia had been booked moments before the goal, Seo Jin-seok was booked deep in stoppage time, and Seoul held on.

Seoul finished with 16 shots to 14 and eight on target to six. They had 64 percent of the ball in the first half and 48 percent in the second, which is the story of the afternoon in two numbers.

## Player Performances

**[[Euller]] (에울레르):**
Two penalties taken two different ways: power for the first, patience for the second. Afterward he explained that the league's goalkeepers have studied how he takes them, so he struck the first without breaking his run-up and, expecting the keeper to move early on the second, waited and picked the other corner. Thirteen league goals, four of them penalties, is a career best, one more than his 12 in 2025. Three shots, all on target, four key passes and the shot that made the third goal. Booked, his third of the season, so no ban. He went off on 84 minutes and said on the broadcast that he had felt a little pain in his tendon (the interpreter said the Achilles) and did not want to risk an injury that would hurt the team. Asked if he was all right, he said he was. The club has said nothing since.

**[[Park Jae-yong]] (박재용):**
Quiet for long spells, then in the right place on 72. Twelve league goals, all from open play, which Interfootball notes makes him the division's top scorer without penalties. Replaced by Kim Hyun on 77.

**[[Osmar]] (오스마르):**
Captain for the day with Park Chang-hwan injured and Kim Oh-kyu out for the season, and the man fouled for the first penalty. Completed 70 of 76 passes and won four aerial duels. He was down after a challenge when Jeonnam scored their first goal, which the commentators said left the middle of the defence short.

**[[Seo Jin-seok]] (서진석):**
The substitution Kim Do-gyun was happiest with. Sent on at half-time to add attacking passes and switches of play, he made three key passes and three interceptions in 53 minutes, and his manager said afterward that he did exactly that. Booked in the last minute.

**[[Min Sung-jun]] (민성준):**
Four saves, the best of them from Mirandinha's dipping shot with the score at 3-1. Could do nothing about either goal.

**[[Baek Ji-woong]] (백지웅):**
Moved to centre-back with Caio out and completed 67 of 70 passes. Still on four yellow cards, one from a ban.

**Valdivia (발디비아, Jeonnam):**
The best player on the pitch for long spells: the cut-back that started the first goal, the pass for the second, and five key passes in all. Ten goals and seven assists this season. Booked late.

**Kim Ju-yeop (김주엽, Jeonnam):**
Gave away both penalties and owned both: he said afterward that he had held Osmar on the first and that the second was a handball, and that his side had lost because of him.

## Fan Reaction

The papers agreed on one headline: the race for second goes to the last day. Sports Chosun and Xportsnews both used some version of it, and Goal Korea called the race "still in the fog." Several outlets noted that Seoul's win had also delayed Suwon Samsung's promotion, which a Seoul draw would have settled on Saturday. Sports Seoul counted 19 goals in Seoul's last three matches, and Xportsnews led its report with the word luck, which Kim Do-gyun used himself.

At Mokdong it was the first cold day of the season. It was the first day of winter on the Korean calendar, Seoul recorded its first ice of the autumn that morning, and it was 9.2°C and clear at kickoff. The 4,506 crowd was, by our count, the fourth-biggest of the season at Mokdong, and Euller thanked them for coming out in the cold. Before kickoff the club showed a live feed from the dressing room on the stadium screen, which Sports-G reported as a first for the K League.

## News and Transfers

- **Park Chang-hwan** tore a ligament in his ankle (the anterior talofibular ligament) at Yongin after a defender stepped on him, Sports Chosun reported. It is not season-ending. Kim Do-gyun said before the match that he expects three to four weeks, so Park could be back for Gyeongnam on November 29 or, failing that, for the playoffs.
- **Caio** was not in the matchday squad. No reason was given. He was taken off at half-time at Yongin after his back pass led to the second goal, and Kim said afterward that his condition had not been good.
- **Kim Oh-kyu** remains out for the season with a knee ligament injury.
- **Discipline:** Euller is on three yellow cards and Baek Ji-woong on four, one from a ban. Son Hyuk-chan served his suspension at Yongin and was on the bench.
- **Suwon FC** beat Gyeongnam 3-0 at home on Saturday afternoon to go three points clear of Seoul in second.
- **Daegu** won 1-0 at Seongnam and are one point behind Seoul, with more goals scored (58 to 57).
- **Suwon Samsung** need a draw at home to Yongin on Sunday to clinch promotion. Cheonan play Busan and Paju play Chungnam Asan the same day.
- **Elsewhere on Saturday:** Gimpo drew 2-2 with Ansan and Hwaseong won 1-0 at Gimhae.
- **Jeonnam** are 16th. The club released a senior player during the week over a drinking, bullying and assault incident, and the league's disciplinary committee continues its hearing on November 17.

## Tactical and Strategic Watch

**Four again, in a 4-3-3.** The new shape put Baek Ji-woong at centre-back next to Osmar and Kim Joo-hwan at left-back, and it held up better than the back four at Yongin until the last 20 minutes. The problem Kim Do-gyun named afterward was not the shape but the ball: losing it in midfield, he said, is what produces the dangerous counter-attacks, and it happened again here.

**The bench did not deliver, and the manager said so.** Seo Jin-seok apart, Kim was openly critical: when Euller came off, he said, the attack ran out of ideas, and the players who came on did not show what they are good at. He wants players who have not played much to take their roles with more confidence. With Park Chang-hwan out and Caio out of the squad, the depth will be tested again.

**Penalties and dead balls.** Both goals that turned the match were penalties, the first won at a corner. Seoul have now scored from set-piece situations in each of their last three matches: a corner and a free kick against Gimhae, Osmar's header from a corner at Yongin, and this.

**The tiebreaker is level.** Seoul and Suwon FC have each scored 57. On equal points, goals scored decides, then goal difference, where Suwon FC lead by seven. Kim said before kickoff that all Seoul can do is win the remaining matches and score as many as possible.

## My Honest Read

Kim Do-gyun was right not to celebrate. This was a win built on two penalties, one of them given after a long look at a monitor, against the side in 16th, and for the last 20 minutes Seoul were hanging on. But it was the result they had to have. Lose this and second place would have been gone with two rounds left; win it and it stays mathematically alive, along with third place, which matters more than it sounds: third plays sixth rather than fifth in the playoff semi-final and would host the final, where the higher seed goes through on a draw. The honest position is that Seoul no longer control second. They need two wins, and they need Suwon FC, who have won two straight since that home defeat to Chungnam Asan, to take no more than three points from Gimpo away and Cheongju at home, and to finish behind Seoul on goals scored if they take exactly three. The part they do control is third, and Daegu are one point behind it.
