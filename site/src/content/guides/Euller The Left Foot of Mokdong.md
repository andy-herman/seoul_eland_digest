Every promotion push has a face. Seoul E-Land's wears the number 7, answers to [[Euller]], and swings the best left foot in K League 2.

## From Paraiba, via everywhere

Elosman Euller Silva Cavalcanti was born on January 4, 1995 in São José de Piranhas, a small town in the Brazilian state of Paraíba. His football education ran through the northeast: youth football in Paraíba, then the academies of Bahia and Vitória in Salvador, where he came up not as a winger but as a left-back. He played nearly a hundred matches for Vitória and won three Campeonato Baiano state titles, and along the way wore Brazil's youth shirts, taking bronze at the 2015 Pan American Games.

Then came the journeyman decade that makes his current form so satisfying: Avispa Fukuoka in Japan, Al-Shabab in Saudi Arabia, back to Brazil with CSA and Ferroviária, AEL Limassol in Cyprus, and finally Portugal, with Chaves and then Marítimo in Madeira, where his numbers as a converted attacker began to spike. In January 2025, Seoul E-Land took him on loan from Marítimo for 200,000 euros with an option to buy. The club announcement called him a left-footed kick specialist. That undersold it.

## The 2025 season that changed everything

Manager Kim Do-gyun's first big call was positional: in preseason he moved Euller to the right wing as an inverted winger, everything bending back onto the left foot, and told reporters that if Euller performed, promotion was possible. What followed was one of the best individual seasons in club history: 11 goals and 11 assists in the league (12 goals across all competitions), the fastest player ever to reach 10 goals and 10 assists in a K League 2 season, eight appearances in the round Best XI, the league lead in key passes, and the 2026 K League 2 **Assist King** title, only the second individual award in Seoul E-Land history. He finished runner-up for league MVP.

The club exercised the purchase option that November, a deal Portuguese media valued at up to a million euros with bonuses, and Euller signed a three-year contract through 2028 on the season's final day, after scoring in a 6-0 win. He turned down interest from K League 1 clubs to stay in the second division. His explanation: "This place is my home."

## 2026: fewer numbers, bigger moments

This season started late for him, a hamstring injury costing him the opening weeks, and the raw stat line (five goals and four assists through Round 18) is quieter than 2025. The moments have not been. In May, on his 50th appearance for the club, he curled in a direct free kick and afterward delivered the quote that now follows him around: "I want to become a player remembered in this club's history. Personal records are not important at all."

After the World Cup break he opened the second half of the season by breaking Gimhae's resistance with a trademark strike, a first-time left-footed hit from outside the box in a 3-1 win, celebrated with a Ronaldo-style siu that the broadcast crew loved (our [[2026-R16_Seoul_E-Land_Digest|Round 16 digest]] has the full story). Two weeks later he scored again in the 2-2 comeback draw at Suwon FC. When E-Land need a door opened, the left foot still gets the first knock.

## What makes him work

Watch him for one match and the pattern is unmistakable. He starts wide right, drifts infield, and turns everything into a left-footed geometry problem: inswinging crosses, reversed passes into the half spaces, shots arced toward the far corner, and set pieces that are a standing threat from anywhere within 25 meters. The stats profile matches the eye test: top of the league in assists and near the top in key passes, crosses, and dribbles in 2025. He is also, by teammates' and his own account, one of the dressing room's glue players, part of a Portuguese-speaking contingent alongside fellow Brazilians Alan Cariús and Gabriel, and quick to praise Korea itself: "The food is amazing, and the Korean people are very polite and helpful."

## The stakes of this autumn

Euller has finished a season 4th and been knocked out in the playoffs. He has heard the noise of a promotion race and watched it end in silence. That is precisely why he stayed: with Seoul E-Land 2nd in the table and two automatic promotion spots on offer in 2026, the player who chose this club over the top flight has a chance to carry it there himself. If it happens, the first name in the story of E-Land's promotion will not need looking up.
